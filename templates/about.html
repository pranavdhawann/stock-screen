About Stock Screen

Market intelligence infrastructure for equity research

WHO WE ARE

Stock Screen is a market intelligence platform that consolidates the research workflow — news aggregation, regulatory filing analysis, sentiment measurement, and price forecasting — into a single interface covering US and Indian equities.

The platform is built for teams and individuals who need defensible inputs quickly: every headline, filing summary, and sentiment score is traceable to its source, and every figure is timestamped so you always know how current the picture is.

WHAT WE DELIVER
Multi-source news aggregation
Company and market-wide coverage consolidated from independent providers, deduplicated and cached server-side for consistent latency.
Regulatory filing analysis
SEC EDGAR and BSE disclosures parsed and summarised into catalysts, risks, and material changes with links back to the source document.
Sentiment measurement
Article-level scoring rolled up into a daily series per symbol, retained as history so you can see how positioning shifted over time.
Technical and forecast signals
Standard indicator sets alongside model-driven price projections, presented with their assumptions rather than as standalone recommendations.
WHO WE SERVE
Research teams
consolidating coverage across a watchlist without maintaining in-house data pipelines.
Active traders
who need a fast, sourced read on a name before committing capital.
Independent investors
seeking institutional-grade research coverage at a proportionate cost.
PLANS

IMPORTANT DISCLOSURE

Stock Screen provides information and analytics only. Nothing on this platform is investment advice, a recommendation, or an offer to buy or sell any security. Data is sourced from third parties and may be delayed or incomplete. Independent verification and your own judgement remain essential.
